# 渗透体视觉签名探索 — 四方向对比

每个方向生成 1-2 张，对比选出最有"游戏签名"感的。

---

## 方向 A：折叠（Folded）

视觉钩子：人形折纸——身体被沿不该存在的线折过，折痕处透光。

```
Top-down view of a humanoid figure, pixel art style, low-fi aesthetic, dark background. The figure is recognizably human -- but its body has been FOLDED along impossible creases. Parts of the torso are creased inward like paper being folded while someone is still inside. The fold lines are perfectly straight, cutting across anatomy without regard for bone or muscle. Where the body folds, the surface splits slightly and faint cyan-teal light leaks through the crease like light through a folded lampshade. One arm is folded back against itself at an angle that breaks physics. The head is slightly creased down the middle as if someone tried to fold it in half but stopped partway. The figure still moves, still walks, but every joint bends along the fold lines rather than natural joints. The clothing and skin have sharp geometric crease marks like origami. Approximately 15-20% of the body shows these fold distortions. Atmosphere of wrongness, impossible physics applied to flesh. high-detail pixel art rendering, extremely dark atmosphere, NOT retro-cute pixel art, NOT bright color palette, no organic horror, no blood, subtle geometric body horror only
```

## 方向 B：偏移复制（Offset Duplication）

视觉钩子：套色错位——身体部件被精确复制并偏移，像没对准的多重曝光。

```
Top-down view of a humanoid figure, pixel art style, low-fi aesthetic, dark background. The figure is recognizably human -- but parts of its body appear to exist in multiple slightly offset copies simultaneously. One hand is actually three overlapping semi-transparent hands, each displaced by a few pixels in different directions. The head has a faint duplicate shifted 2-3 pixels to the left, like a misaligned print registration. One leg appears to stutter in space -- the same leg repeated at slightly different positions along its stride. Where the copies overlap, the color intensifies into a faint cyan-teal glow. The core body is solid dark grey, but its edges are smeared by these ghost-copies. The duplicates are not random -- they are precisely offset, mathematically regular, like a printing error that follows exact rules. Approximately 15-20% of the body shows this offset effect. Atmosphere of spatial glitch, reality failing to render this being correctly. high-detail pixel art rendering, extremely dark atmosphere, NOT retro-cute pixel art, NOT bright color palette, no organic horror, chromatic aberration on a body, misregistration horror
```

## 方向 C：平面切割（Planar Slicing）

视觉钩子：被 grid 切割的人——身体沿无视解剖的直线被切开重组，缝隙发光。

```
Top-down view of a humanoid figure, pixel art style, low-fi aesthetic, dark background. The figure is recognizably human -- but its body has been CUT along perfectly straight lines that ignore anatomy completely. A diagonal line runs from left shoulder to right hip, and the two halves are misaligned by a few pixels -- the top half shifted slightly left of where the bottom half expects it. Another horizontal cut across the thighs has caused the legs to be offset forward. The cuts are impossibly clean and straight -- not wounds, not tears, but geometric slices as if reality itself was sectioned along a grid. In the gaps between misaligned sections, thin lines of cyan-teal light glow steadily. The figure still holds together despite the misalignment, moving as one piece but visibly WRONG in its assembly. Approximately 15-20% of the body shows these slice-and-shift distortions. Atmosphere of geometric dissection, surgical precision by something that does not understand human bodies. high-detail pixel art rendering, extremely dark atmosphere, NOT retro-cute pixel art, NOT bright color palette, no organic horror, no blood, clean geometric cuts only
```

## 方向 D：局部坍缩（Dimensional Collapse）

视觉钩子：降维幸存者——身体部位被压扁成低维投影，3D 变 2D。

```
Top-down view of a humanoid figure, pixel art style, low-fi aesthetic, dark background. The figure is recognizably human -- but parts of its body have COLLAPSED into lower dimensions. One arm has been flattened into what looks like a 2D shadow or projection pressed against the side of the torso -- it still moves but has no depth, like a paper cutout attached to a 3D body. One foot is just a line -- the entire volume compressed into a single axis. A section of the chest appears to be a flat geometric diagram of what a chest WOULD look like if projected from 3D to 2D -- anatomical cross-section visible as a surface texture. Where 3D meets 2D, the transition zone glows faint cyan-teal as spatial dimensions blend unstably. The remaining 3D parts of the body look normal but isolated, surrounded by their own flattened projections. Approximately 15-20% of the body shows dimensional collapse. Atmosphere of spatial impossibility, physics breaking down at a personal scale. high-detail pixel art rendering, extremely dark atmosphere, NOT retro-cute pixel art, NOT bright color palette, no organic horror, dimensional reduction horror, flatland meets flesh
```

## 评审对比标准

生成后横向对比四个方向：

1. **签名感**：哪个一眼就是"只有这个游戏才有"？
2. **32px 可读性**：缩到游戏尺寸后，这个变异特征还能看出来吗？
3. **等级递进潜力**：这个概念能从 15%（渗透体）自然演进到 50%（改写体）到 100%（覆盖体）吗？
4. **不可名状感**：看到后大脑会不会持续尝试"理解"但无法归类？
5. **与环境的一致性**：这种变异方式和裂隙环境（碎片拼接、空间错位）在叙事上能呼应吗？
