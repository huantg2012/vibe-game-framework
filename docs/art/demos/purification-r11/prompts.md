# R11 第一阶段构图生成记录

工具：内置 image_gen。三张独立新生成图片，无输入图像；下列尺寸与比例来自当前显示事实，旧画面未作为形体/画法参考。

图像用途：完整灰阶构图探索，不是成品画质样板或已接入游戏资产。正常视口 3:2，人物本体约占全高 5.2%。

## A · 断壁深隙

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: FIRST-STAGE GAME COMPOSITION STUDY, one complete scene, landscape 1536x1024, aspect ratio 3:2. This is a thoughtfully composed grayscale value study by a senior environment concept artist, using 6-8 broad value groups, confident angular masses, sparse medium-size construction cues, clean silhouette edges, restrained brushwork. NO color, NO pixel texture, NO microdetail, NO noise, NO HUD, NO typography, NO labels, NO border, NO montage. A small maintained refuge is embedded INSIDE the cut-open enclosure of a heavy ancient industrial structure, surrounded by an enormous dark anomalous world. The refuge must feel like a room with back/side walls and a load-bearing roof shown cut away for play, not a floating island, not a display plinth, not a freestanding open balcony. Shallow oblique game camera looking slightly downward; two walkable floor elevations connected by two broad continuous ramps, no jumping. Keep usable interior floor depth and a continuous open circulation loop, with meaningful asymmetry and grouped equipment. Include exactly ONE tiny hooded worker; their opaque body height is 5.2% of the entire image height, the fixed scale reference. Include exactly SIX recognizable grouped equipment silhouettes: a vertical open split containment core (height 22% of image) with a small pale interior as the primary focus; a waist-high deep cylindrical storage vessel (10.5%); a low boxy purifier with visible filtration cavities (10%); a standing human-sized containment capsule (14%); a rounded pressure ring/closed vessel on support (11%); a dark jagged wound in the floor at the departure edge (foreshortened vertical footprint 7%). Their heights and the worker scale must stay fixed; do not miniaturize these to make the environment huge. Equipment belongs against appropriate walls, embedded supports or service bays, never six items evenly spaced on pedestals. Architecture has substantial visible undersides, thick broken structural cores and thin wall skins, not repeated parallel beveled stripes. Establish near connected ruins, a dim middle structure with comprehensible scale and overlap, and a barely distinguishable very distant mass. Main focal hierarchy: core and worker, refuge construction, mid-distance interruption, then unresolvable depth. Most exterior is quiet; keep a few decisive edges where they describe overlap/plane changes. Darkness is a shaped volume between structures, not an empty background with decorative fragments around its perimeter. Avoid cathedral, temple, fantasy castle, ornate machinery, cyberpunk, heroic sunbeams, uniform rimlight, repeated zigzag ribbons, an all-purpose fog glow. Static depth must be clear even at thumbnail size.

COMPOSITION A — REFUGE IN A SHEARED WALL. Position refuge in lower-left, filling roughly the left 57% width and lower 60% height; worker near (34%,76%), core foot at (26%,74%), storage (13%,70%), purifier (44%,70%), standing capsule on upper level (20%,49%), pressure vessel on upper level (39%,50%), departure wound near (53%,64%). Enclose the refuge within the enormous thickness of a diagonal load-bearing wall that enters from the cropped upper-left and continues behind the room toward the upper-right middle distance. The broken right wall and a few roots reveal this shared construction rather than adding a decorative ring. Let a substantial near fractured buttress descend from the refuge's rear/right foundation toward image right, with a visible short end face, stopping before it encloses the dark gap. Across an oblique black gulf, show one large partially occluded middle-distance wall/tower around (77%,40%), tiny secondary apertures indicating a much larger scale. The diagonal viewing path goes core -> rear/right opening -> truncated middle wall -> one dim far plane above/right. A foreground cut-off structural fragment at lower-right partly occludes the middle gulf but never forms a continuous frame. Generous unequal voids, believable asymmetrical support, dramatic compression at left opening into depth at right. The void must continue behind foreground mass and beyond the canvas.
```
