Create one landscape PIXEL ART concept sheet for the game "那天之后 / AFTER THAT DAY", an already-ruined cosmic-horror solitary scavenging game. This is a design exploration, NOT production sprite assets. The actual game has a tiny, anonymous brown-coated survivor wearing a round brown hood/helmet, charcoal trousers, muted ochre leather backpack and a tiny amber SHOULDER lamp, on damp dark concrete. Pure 2D overhead three-quarter game perspective, discrete hand-placed pixel clusters, low resolution objects enlarged by nearest-neighbor aesthetic. No smooth 3D shading, no painterly blur, no anime, no medieval heroic fantasy, no big guns or neon. Materials are oily dark iron, weathered brown binding, stained canvas, dull bone-gray ceramic. The world is polluted by rewritten physical rules, not tentacle decoration everywhere.
Composition: a restrained charcoal field split into FOUR generous vertical columns, each with a large standalone weapon near top (about 45% height) and THREE very small consistent survivor action silhouettes near bottom showing windup, contact, recovery. All four full weapons and all silhouettes must fit. Column labels ONLY: "01 短撬", "02 楔头长杆", "03 配重锤", "04 钉射器". Small top title "那天之后 · 武器构想". No other explanatory text. Numbers should be readable.
01 SHORT PRY: a compact iron wrecking/pry bar, approx forearm length, flat split wedge on one end, worn cloth grip, a repaired collar with a tiny pale ceramic seam. Practical hooked silhouette, not a sword. Bottom survivor action: short committed side strike, weight stays low, then retract.
02 WEDGE POLE: long wooden/metal maintenance rod, approx survivor height, slender dark shaft with cloth repair, flattened asymmetric wedge tip, no enormous spearhead. Bottom: two-handed brace, a narrow straight thrust, then pull back without huge sword slash.
03 COUNTERWEIGHT HAMMER: short two-handed shaft and a dense asymmetric rectangular salvaged elevator counterweight head, one worn ceramic retaining strip; heavy but credible to carry, not a fantasy sledge twice the body size. Bottom: shift weight back, downward diagonal impact, heavy recovery.
04 SINGLE-SHOT NAIL DRIVER: compact industrial pistol tool with short square nose, hand-loading breech, small mechanical pressure cylinder and lever, one visible thick iron fastener lying next to it. No magazine, no assault rifle stock, no glowing magic beam. Bottom: stabilize with both hands, one abrupt shot with tiny gray dust puff, then visibly open breech.
Palette restrained soot black, desaturated iron gray, linen taupe, old brown; tiny amber shoulder light and a single extremely subdued green-gray anomalous seam per weapon at most. Silhouettes distinct at game size, materials intimate and repaired. Everything must read as surviving labor equipment being used reluctantly as weapons. Give objects sufficient contrast to judge silhouettes without turning them into polished merchandise. Keep the sheet visually serious, spacious, beautiful and credible for this specific low-fi horror world.
