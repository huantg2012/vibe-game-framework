Asset: NEW weapon-quality concept sheet for the pixel-art cosmic horror survival game 那天之后 / After That Day. This is a major corrective redesign. The user rejected a progression of increasingly engineered steel tools and ceramic armor. Quality must now read as progressively stronger ALIEN REWRITING / CORROSIVE METAMORPHOSIS OF AN ORDINARY CROWBAR. Form changes, not manufactured upgrades, not recoloring.

World: the catastrophe already happened. Alien patterns overwrite matter and causality; familiar objects persist in physically wrong but stable arrangements. The protagonist scavenges usable remnants, not superhero weapons. These are four qualities of the SAME close-range crowbar family, ONE attack mode. Maintain a recognizable small hooked pry end and a graspable lower shaft throughout. Same overall effective length and hand-to-working-tip reach for all four. Do NOT change them into axes, swords, staves, scythes or hammers. Vary actual SILHOUETTES, curves, asymmetry, negative spaces, shoulder topology and distribution of matter, not merely surface rust or decoration. The four should be individually identifiable in monochrome thumbnails.

Wide horizontal four-column sheet on worn near-black charcoal cloth. All four same scale, same lighting, similar downward diagonal display angle, fully visible and well spaced. Title 那天之后 · 撬棍. Headings 普通 / 优良 / 精良 / 卓越. Main items take 65% of sheet height. Beneath them a row of small same-design thumbnail versions, no extra stats or prose. More corrupt and unusual from left to right, but progressively compelling and desirable as rare game loot. This is not a 'more damaged = less usable' durability chart.

1 普通 — An unaltered, narrow, single-piece iron crowbar, recognizable rounded J hook, nearly straight narrow shaft and short forked toe. Dark old iron, warm oxidized patches, small dirty cloth grip. Simple human object. Plain steel tool, modest wear, sound and usable.

2 优良 — First stable invasive rewriting. Retains the iron crowbar almost entirely, but the HOOK ROOT has swollen unevenly to one side and a short second blunt iron lip has grown fused behind the working hook, creating a distinctive crooked offset shoulder. Upper shaft has a mild asymmetric dog-leg; lower graspable shaft stays straight. A continuous dark mineral seam appears to squeeze out of the metal along one side. Local dry rust-crust, faint dead verdigris caught INSIDE cracks; warm iron still dominates. One clear asymmetric upper silhouette change, not many little spikes. No manufactured collar or ornate attachments.

3 精良 — The IRON ITSELF has folded and partially split longitudinally. Crooked S-shaped upper shaft, a conspicuous narrow irregular WINDOW of empty darkness between two fused iron strands; the strands braid back together before the hand grip. Hook root is two offset corroded laminae that rejoin into ONE working hooked tip. Several compressed shelf-like folds of dry black metal, one-sided expansion, sharply uneven contour. This looks like a crowbar retained as the memory of a thing while alien topology rearranges it. Broad chipped iron planes and subdued green-grey mineral interior, with a little pale oxidation on torn inner surfaces. Keep metal identity, no flesh organs or rubber tentacles. More memorable silhouette and negative space than quality 2.

4 卓越 — Visibly impossible but compact and usable corrupted crowbar. Lower hand grip is the last plain human iron section with a little old binding. The upper shaft has been REWRITTEN INTO THREE CONNECTED, OFFSET, CURLING IRON FOLDS around a long crooked void: it seems partly folded through its own volume. The upper hook is an irregular OPEN HOLLOW KNUCKLE of fused black-iron plates, its negative space uneven and displaced, converging on a short blunt hooked working tip. A dark split core seems deeper than the width of the object. Asymmetric repeated planes, displaced edges, eroded mineral inner surfaces, darkness caught between compressed layers. One dominant eerie shape, not a Christmas tree of decoration. Most of the mass is dark iron; muted grey-green exposed inner strata and sparse bone-grey weathering clarify the impossible folds without looking like a fitted white shell. It should feel like a fragment of another world's matter that happens to still accept a human hand. Stable continuous connections, no detached hovering fragments. Exceptional through ALIEN FORM, not through gold, machined finish, glowing gems, armor plating or bigger attack reach.

Rendering: handcrafted hard-edge PIXEL ART concept, consistent visible square clusters, controlled coarse pixels, 4-5 material value bands, selective sparse dither, legible major shapes. Directional soft dim light, NO smooth 3D renders, NO glossy product render. Grounded post-cosmic-horror equipment with eerie surviving human familiarity. No eye symbols, skulls, bones as handles, spikes, neon auras, elemental fire, sci-fi grip panels, artificial circuitry, or decorative runes. No automatic scene lighting based on rarity; same light for all. Micro teal stains only within fractures, not a color-coded rarity glow. Quality 3/4 must have stronger actual silhouette and negative-space changes than quality 1/2. Labels are plain Chinese sans serif. Only title and four quality names.
